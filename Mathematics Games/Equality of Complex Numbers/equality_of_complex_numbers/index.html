        How to Play

        The goal

        Escape five chambers. Each door shows an inscription such as 3x + i7 = 15 + i7. Two complex numbers are equal only when their real parts are equal and their imaginary parts are equal.

        1. Sort the terms

        Drag each stone tablet onto the Sun Seal (real part: no i) or the Moon Seal (imaginary part: the number multiplied by i). You can also tap a tablet, then tap a seal. i7 means 7i: its imaginary part is 7.

        2. Solve

        When both seals are set, solve the two equations for x (and y). Type whole numbers with the keyboard or the stone keypad; use − for negatives. Press the button or Enter.

        The hourglass

        Rooms last 30, 40, 50, 60 and 75 seconds. A wrong solution or an empty hourglass resets the room with a new inscription after 3 seconds.

        Coins and stars

        Opening a door pays 100 coins, plus 4 per second left, minus 15 per hint and 10 per mistake (never below 20). A mistake is a tablet on the wrong seal, a wrong solution or a time-out. Stars: 3 with no mistakes, 2 with 1–2, 1 with 3 or more.

        Hints and the free helper

        The Eye button shows a hint for 15 coins: it places a tablet while sorting, or shows a working step while solving. When half the time has gone, the scarab helps for free: the next tablet and its seal pulse, or the first working step appears.

        Keys: Enter to solve, Esc or P to pause, digits and − to type.

        Learn More: Equality of Complex Numbers

        The rule

        a + ib = c + id  ⇔  a = c and b = d

        A complex number has two independent parts. The real axis and the imaginary axis never mix, so one equation between complex numbers is really two equations between real numbers. That is why one inscription can fix two unknowns, x and y.

        i7 and 7i are the same number. In i(4y + 3) the whole bracket is the imaginary part.

        How the rooms grow

        • Room 1: the imaginary parts already match; solve for x.
        • Rooms 2–3: each part holds one unknown.
        • Room 4: the real part gives x; substitute it into the imaginary part.
        • Room 5: both parts hold x and y; solve the system by elimination.
