How to Play

Your objective: reach the summit of the Ascent by solving every gravity riddle.

  • Ascend. Tap a numbered node on the Ascent Map to face its riddle. Nodes unlock in order.
  • Answer. Read the riddle, then tap one of the nine answer tiles. Wrong tiles dim out — keep going until you find the truth.
  • Earn. Each node solved for the first time awards 100 Star Shards.
  • Hint. Stuck? Spend 30 Star Shards for a clue — one hint per node.
  • Unlock. A correct answer opens that node's Codex: an interactive simulation you can play with.
  • Controls. Tap or click anywhere. On a keyboard, use 1–9 to pick an answer, H for a hint, and Esc to go back.
