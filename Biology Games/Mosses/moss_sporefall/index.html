HOW TO PLAY

You are a moss spore. Drift down through the forest and land on damp ground. Gather every glowing Dew Orb on the way down — each one carries a fact about mosses.

CONTROLS

  • DRAG Touch and drag anywhere on the screen to steer the spore.
  • On-screen pads, or the arrow keys / A and D, drift left and right.
  • DIVE Hold the dive pad or press Down / S to fall faster.
  • P Pause the descent. Esc also works.

WHAT TO CHASE, WHAT TO DODGE

Dew Orb
Collect it. Scores points and reveals a moss fact. Collect them in a row for a bonus.
Moisture Drop
Restores one point of moisture. One hides in every zone.
Impostors
Ferns, cones and flowers are not mosses. Touching one costs moisture.
Rock Spurs
Dry stone jutting from the canyon wall. Squeeze past them.

MOISTURE

You start each descent with three . Lose them all and the spore dries out before it can germinate. Brushing the canyon walls is safe.

THE MOSS FIELD JOURNAL

Mosses are bryophytes: small, non-vascular land plants with no true roots, no flowers and no seeds. They were the first plants to leave the water, and they still need a film of it to reproduce.
