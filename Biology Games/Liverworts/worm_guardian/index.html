How to Play

Controls & objective only

Your objective

Guide the guardian worm across the forest floor and gather every drifting sporophyte spore. Each spore you reach opens a field note. Clear all spores in a stage to advance. Clear all three stages to complete the sporophyte and win.

Collect spores

Touch a glowing spore to absorb it. +10 score, one field note — and the worm grows longer with every spore it swallows.

Avoid the insects

Ladybugs, ants and spiders roam the floor — and some hunt you. A red tracking ring marks a hunter. Each hit costs one life. You have three.

Dash to survive

A dash makes you briefly fast and untouchable — the safe way past a hunter or through a tight gap.

Lose all lives

Run out and the stage is lost. Progress you already unlocked is kept.

    Liverworts — reference & definitions

    Liverworts (division Marchantiophyta) are among the simplest land plants — small, rootless bryophytes that live tethered to moisture. They have no true roots, stems, leaves, or vascular tissue.

    Key terms

    Common examples

    • Marchantia — the classic thalloid liverwort, easily recognised by its gemma cups.
    • Riccia — a small thalloid liverwort of damp soil and still water.
    • Porella — a leafy liverwort with leaf‑like structures in two rows.
    • Pellia — a simple thalloid liverwort of wet stream banks.

    The life cycle at a glance

    Why it matters

    Liverworts are close to the earliest plants to colonise land. Because they depend on a film of water for fertilisation, they never stray far from damp ground — which is exactly why you find them on stream banks, shaded rocks and tree bark. They also act as pioneer species, holding moisture and building the thin soil that larger plants later need.

    About the obstacles

    The ladybug, ant and spider in this game are obstacles, not biology lessons — they represent the everyday hazards of the forest floor. Contact with one costs the guardian a life.
