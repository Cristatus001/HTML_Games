HOW TO PLAY

You are the Cell Cycle Controller. Each card is a live cell awaiting your ruling. Read its conditions, pick one regulatory action, and keep the genome stable to the end of the shift.

OBJECTIVE

  • Judge every card in the case file correctly.
  • A wrong ruling costs 25% Genome Stability.
  • If stability hits 0% the shift ends in apoptosis.
  • Finish above 50% for a clean cycle.

THE FOUR ACTIONS

ALLOWLet the cycle proceed untouched.
PAUSEArrest progression so slow processes catch up.
REPAIRTrigger corrective machinery to fix or finish DNA.
DESTROYOrder apoptosis when damage is beyond saving.

CONTROLS

  • Tap or click an action button.
  • Keyboard: 1 Allow   2 Pause   3 Repair   4 Destroy
  • Enter or Space advances to the next card.
  • Esc pauses the shift or closes a panel.

Reference biology, definitions and case notes live behind LEARN MORE on the debrief screen.
