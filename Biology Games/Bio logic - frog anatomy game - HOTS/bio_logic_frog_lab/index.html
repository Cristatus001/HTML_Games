HOW TO PLAY

OBJECTIVE

Work through 10 case studies on the anatomy of the frog. Each case describes a situation and offers four possible explanations — choose the one that is physiologically correct. One point per correct call, and your rank at the end depends on the total.

CONTROLS

  • TAP / CLICK Select an answer, or press any button.
  • 1234 Choose option A, B, C or D from the keyboard.
  • ENTER Skip the wait and advance to the next case.
  • ESC Close any open panel.

SCORING

After every answer the console explains the reasoning and holds for 8 seconds so you can read it — press NEXT CASE to move on sooner. A wrong call cannot be retaken in the same session, so read the scenario carefully before committing.
