How to play

Objective

Work down the burrow from the surface hole to the blind end. Each of the five chambers asks one riddle about hemichordate anatomy. Answer it and the worm crawls on to the next chamber. Clear all five to file your report.

Controls

  • Type your answer into the answer field.
  • Enter or Check answer submits it.
  • Hint reveals a clue for the current chamber — it costs grains.
  • Everything responds to touch exactly as it does to a mouse.

Grains and rating

  • A chamber cleared first try pays 100 grains.
  • Using the hint drops it to 60; each wrong answer costs 20 more.
  • A chamber never pays less than 20.
  • Your grain total sets the cast rating out of three.
