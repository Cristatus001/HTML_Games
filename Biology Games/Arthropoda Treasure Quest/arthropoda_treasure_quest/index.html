How to Play

Objective, Explorer
  1. 1Tap any treasure chest on the map to dig it up and reveal a question about arthropod biology.
  2. 2Study the clue and specimen photo, then tap one of the four answer tabs. Mouse, touch and stylus all work.
  3. 3A correct answer bursts the chest open with gold and banks a point. A wrong answer cracks it open empty and reveals the right answer, so you still learn it.
  4. 4Dig up every chest, in any order, to complete the expedition. Your time is kept for the record book — it never runs out.
