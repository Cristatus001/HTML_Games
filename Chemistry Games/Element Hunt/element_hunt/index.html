How to Play

Controls & objective

  • Read the clue. The panel gives you a hint plus the element's period and group.
  • Find the symbol hidden in the 15 × 15 letter grid. Symbols run across, down or diagonally.
  • Capitals matter. A symbol is written exactly as on the periodic table — He, never HE or he. The grid is full of both cases, so strike the right one.
  • Drag to strike. Press on the first letter of the symbol and drag to the last, then release — in reading order. Dragging it backwards does not count. Touch and mouse both work.
  • Correct: the symbol is struck through and you score 10 points first try, 5 after that, plus up to +5 for speed.
  • Wrong: the grid shakes and you lose 2 points. Keep hunting - specimens must be found in order.
  • Stuck? A hint reveals the first letter for 5 points. Clear all 15 specimens to open the next vault.
  • Really stuck? After 45 seconds the symbol starts beating on the board until you strike it — free, no points lost.
