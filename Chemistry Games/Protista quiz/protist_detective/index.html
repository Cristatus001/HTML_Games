How to Play

Your objective, Detective
  1. 1A specimen appears under the lens with a written field clue describing its traits.
  2. 2Read the clue, then tap one of the answer tabs to identify the organism or fact. Mouse, touch and stylus all work.
  3. 3A correct identification stamps the plate green and earns 10 points. A wrong one reveals the right answer so you still learn it.
  4. 4Press Analyse Next Sample to move on. Work through the casebook to earn your rank and stars.

Tip: keywords in the clue are your strongest evidence.
