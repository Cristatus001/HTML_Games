MISSION BRIEFING

Your station's resources obey the Law of Conservation of Mass: nothing is created or destroyed, only converted. Wastewater and CO₂ build up as the crew lives and breathes.

PURIFY WATER converts wastewater into clean H₂O (plus a little solid waste).

REVITALIZE AIR converts CO₂ into breathable O₂ (plus a little solid waste).

Keep water and oxygen above zero until the mission clock runs out. Watch for solar flares, micrometeoroid strikes, and system malfunctions — and grab floating supply bonuses for extra score.

Every mission escalates through 3 PHASES — demands and emergencies increase as time goes on. Watch the CURRENT OBJECTIVE panel for bonus mission goals, and act fast when a quick-decision alert appears — it won't wait for you.

Each processor needs a short cooldown after use, so you can't spam both at once — recycle whichever resource is glowing to prioritize the one your station needs most right now.

Tap or click the processor buttons whenever a resource runs low. Good luck, Commander.
