How To Play
  • Drag along the bottom line to move your spaceship (or press A / D).
  • Tap either Shoot button to fire (or press Spacebar).
  • The question tab tells you which hybridization to shoot down.
  • You have 3 lives. Shooting the wrong compound costs a life and 5 points. A missed target only costs 5 points — no life lost.
  • Survive the 60-second timer (or reach 15 correct hits) to complete the run.
  • Chain correct answers to earn power-ups: Sure Hit, Slow Time, and Time Freeze.
